# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Consultants who work alone and need to see what is in progress, how long work really takes, and how many hours went into which task. They use Boto on their own Mac, all day, between client work. Their data stays on the machine.

## Product Purpose
A personal Kanban app for macOS: projects hold boards, boards hold lists, lists hold cards. It adds flow metrics (WIP limits, throughput, cycle time, cumulative flow), a timesheet and report, and Apple Calendar for due dates. Success is that the person can answer "what am I working on, how long does it take, and what did I bill" without leaving the app or sending data anywhere.

## Brand Personality
Calm and focused. Precise and technical. The work comes first; the interface stays quiet and states numbers plainly.

## Anti-references

## Design Principles
- The work is the content. Chrome stays quiet, and colour signals state (blocked, overdue, done), not decoration.
- Numbers are stated plainly. Metrics show the figure, the unit and the period, with no embellishment.
- Dark is a decision, not a default. The interface is dark on purpose and does not follow the system appearance.
- Local first. Nothing leaves the Mac unless the person uses a feature that says so, and the interface makes that visible.
- Destructive actions are never one stray click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirement beyond macOS good practice. In practice that means every control has a spoken name, text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast on its surface, and motion respects Reduce Motion. The audit of 2026-10-27 scored accessibility 1 of 4, so these are open work, not achieved state.
